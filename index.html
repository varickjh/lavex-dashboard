Notes
  • All data are weighted.
  • Interpret proportions and percentages with caution when the cell size is less than 100.
  • We cannot assume that differences are statistically significant.
  • Hover over a bar to see the raw count behind it.
  • Charts can be downloaded as a PNG with the camera icon in the chart toolbar.
  • On default, the app shows data for all of Louisiana, for both survey years (2023 and 2025).
  • Each year uses its own survey weight: the 2023 state-representative weight, and the 2025 pooled LA/MS/TX weight that the 2025 report recommends.
  • A comparison that was not collected in a year (for example Louisiana Region in 2023) shows bars for the other year only.
